**Field** is a visual research space.  
A collection of works situated between photography, digital construction, and graphic intuition.

Each image operates as a fragment — not illustrative, not explanatory — but as a state: residue, tension, drift, suspension.  
The project embraces ambiguity, hybrid processes, and quiet distortion, allowing images to exist without hierarchy or narrative obligation.

Field is not a series.  
It is a condition.
